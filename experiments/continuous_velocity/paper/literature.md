# Related work and defensible contribution boundary

Checked 2026-10-13 against primary papers and author resources. This is a targeted review, not an exhaustive novelty search. Differences below are our interpretation of the cited methods, not priority claims made by their authors.

The candidate contribution is **fitting one continuous motion parameter from learned correspondences, then reusing it to align partial observations and directly query a fixed appearance memory at future times**, with controlled interventions to identify which parts help. The current implementation first performs spatial correspondence matching, then fits velocity. The fitted velocity does not feed back into or constrain matching; only the initial displacement search bounds reflect a speed bound. Continuous velocity, differentiable warping, appearance reuse, and trajectory attention individually have substantial prior art. A method paper needs a measurable benefit from the coupling, beyond a strong template-free geometric baseline.

| Closest prior work | What is already established | Consequence for our claim |
|---|---|---|
| [FloWM, Lillemark et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.01075v2) | Structured memory with self-motion compensation and external-motion flow equivariance. The MNIST instantiation has 25 velocity channels, shared recurrent convolutions, integer transport, and max readout. Its discussion explicitly identifies continuous velocity families as future work. | We cannot claim the idea of flow-structured memory or suggest FloWM lacks a continuous-flow formulation. Our candidate advance is a concrete observation-conditioned continuous-velocity implementation and evidence for its benefits and limits. |
| [DNA/CDNA/STP, Finn et al., 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.07157) | Video prediction reuses observed appearance through learned pixel-motion distributions or affine spatial transformations. | “Predict motion instead of regenerating appearance” is not new. Shared motion across a persistent, partially observed memory needs separate evidence. |
| [Skip Connection Neural Advection, Ebert et al., 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v78/frederik%20ebert17a/frederik%20ebert17a.pdf) | Temporal skip connections preserve visual information through occlusion and improve video-based manipulation. | Retaining earlier appearance through occlusion is not sufficient novelty. Our constant moving coordinate system is a specific hypothesis to test, including controlled reappearance. |
| [Motionformer, Patrick et al., 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.05392) | Trajectory attention aggregates video information along implicitly determined motion paths for action recognition. | Avoid claiming trajectory-aligned attention itself as new. Our memory-fusion restriction is an explicit physical trajectory shared with future prediction; the initial correspondence attention remains unconstrained by its fitted velocity. |
| [GMFlow, Xu et al., 2022](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.13680v3) | Feature correlation followed by softmax matching gives differentiable, subpixel correspondences; attention improves feature matching and flow propagation. | Our correlation-softmax correspondence layer is not a new attention primitive. | [Spatial Transformer Networks, Jaderberg et al., 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02025) | Learned spatial transformations with differentiable sampling can be included in neural architectures. | Bilinear continuous transport is an established building block. | [Vid-ODE, Park et al., 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.08188) | Continuous-time video generation handles irregular frame timing and queries inside and beyond the observed range. | Arbitrary-time prediction is not new; our strict constant-velocity model should earn its simpler parameterization through controlled extrapolation and data-efficiency results. |

## A falsifiable method claim

For single rigid objects with approximately constant velocity and known camera translation, a continuous-motion latent inferred from correspondences and shared by memory alignment and rendering can improve velocity interpolation and future transport without a bank of velocity hypotheses. Whether learning correspondences, learning memory weights, or enforcing the motion law provides additional benefits is empirical. The current code does not implement velocity-conditioned correspondence refinement.

The physical core is weighted least squares,

\[
\hat{\mathbf v}=\frac{\sum_j w_j\Delta t_j\mathbf d_j}{\sum_jw_j\Delta t_j^2},\qquad
\hat I(t,\mathbf x)=M\bigl(\mathbf x+\mathbf c_t-(t-t_0)\hat{\mathbf v}\bigr).
\]

This is a standard estimator connected to a differentiable sampler. Its algebra is not a new theorem. For a fixed velocity the coordinate offset obeys the composition law \((s+t)\mathbf v=s\mathbf v+t\mathbf v\). This law concerns coordinates; repeated bilinear resampling of raster images does not form an exact translation group. Directly querying the same canonical memory avoids repeated resampling of its output, while finite spatial resolution and observation-rendering mismatch remain.

The present implementation uses one global motion component, bounded correspondence searches, known integer camera positions, and a periodic 50-pixel world. It does not inherit FloWM's multi-velocity scene representation or establish equivalence to its full general flow theory. A claim of superior architecture requires a FloWM baseline trained on matched continuous-velocity data; a frozen checkpoint is a motivation/control comparison only.

## Evidence that would change the conclusion

- If raw image correlation plus the same fit and renderer matches the learned model, the result supports geometric structure more strongly than learned attention. Add appearance noise, occlusion, and ambiguity only as predeclared robustness tasks, not as post-hoc attempts to manufacture a gain.
- If quantizing the same inferred velocity worsens performance with all other components fixed, this supports a causal role for transport resolution within this architecture. It does not prove that velocity discretization is the sole cause of the original FloWM failures.
- If mean fusion matches learned fusion, memory attention is unnecessary in the measured regime. Do not bundle its effect with feature or decoder learning.
- If a trained MLP velocity regressor or relaxed motion control catches up, a remaining claim may concern optimization, sample efficiency, or extrapolation. It needs matching learning curves and validation-based selection.
- If rendering transfer fails but oracle velocity repairs it, inference sensitivity is a plausible bottleneck. The oracle intervention does not establish a unique causal explanation because memory alignment changes at the same time.
- If repeated training seeds or long horizons reveal unstable gains, report them directly. A narrow single-object result cannot establish a general world-model contribution.

## Evaluation restrictions relevant to publication

In evaluation schema 2, primary image outcomes are MSE and frame-localization success: the fraction of sufficiently visible target frames with a present detection, no detected duplication, and per-frame periodic localization error within 2 pixels. This periodic measure cannot detect whole-world winding errors. Continuity ADE separately tracks unwrapped positions from a single last-context anchor under bounded increments; irrecoverable winding ambiguity is reported through coverage. Legacy global constant-velocity fits are retained only as explicitly CV-certified diagnostics because they can reject correct frames and nonconstant predicted motion. The known template is supplied only to the evaluator. A successful localization can still contain blurred or fragmented appearance; image metrics and perfect-prediction measurement ceilings are essential.

Oracle velocity interventions are diagnostic references, not guaranteed lower bounds on image error: learned appearance components may compensate for motion-estimation bias. The 2D test's seen and held-out directions do not have matched speed magnitudes. Within-split physical-versus-MLP comparisons and absolute held-out performance are interpretable, but a seen-versus-held-out difference cannot be attributed solely to direction without a matched-speed control.

The fixed detector's synthetic calibration must accompany any ghost claim. Closely overlapping copies are a specific blind spot, so the admissible wording is “no detected ghosts,” rather than “no ghosts.” Test-sequence bootstrap intervals and variation over independently trained models answer different questions and must remain separate. The paired bootstrap uses the same pooled ratio-of-sums estimator as the reported aggregate rates.
